The per-mesh source map is at `/anatomy/source-mesh-mapping.json`. The model was changed only by packing visual normal vectors; positions, triangles and names are preserved. Its source attribution, share-alike terms, original source links, transformation script and checksums are documented at `docs/ANATOMY_MODEL.md` in Dheepika Lab's source repository. The model is illustrative and cannot identify a patient's painful tissue.
